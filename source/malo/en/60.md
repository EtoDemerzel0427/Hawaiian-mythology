{p:320}

¶1 We have a fragment of tradition regarding *Haloa*. The first born son of Wakea was of premature birth (*keiki alualu*) and was given the name of *Haloa-naka*. The little thing died, however, and its body was buried in the ground at one end of the house. After a while from the child's body shot up a taro plant, the leaf of which was named *lau-kapa-lili*, quivering leaf; but the stem was given the name *Haloa*.

¶2 After that another child was born to them, whom they called *Haloa*, from the stalk of the taro. He is the progenitor of all the peoples of the earth.
